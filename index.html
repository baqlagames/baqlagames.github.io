A few things
to know.

Which games are in Baqla?

Texas Hold’em, Color Clash, Rummy and Dutch. Switch between four different card games in the same world.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Multiplayer includes quick matches, private rooms and joining a room by its code. You can also play against bots in single-player.

What can I customize?

Your avatar, card skin, hand and background. The gallery shows the default look alongside different cosmetics you can collect and equip.

Where can I find release updates?

We’re preparing Baqla’s next App Store release. Follow BaqlaGames on Instagram or TikTok for release news and new gameplay.
